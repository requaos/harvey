## 3. Candidate verdicts (the three recorded + the audit's new evidence)

**C1 — the multi-pid wake hook walking a 512-slot PROC_TABLE in IRQ context: EXONERATED as a contention/corruption mechanism on x86_64 (structural).** The walk exists (`pipe_wake`, proc.rs:2126) and runs in IRQ context, but it is uncontended BY CONSTRUCTION: SYSCALL entry clears IF via the hardware FMASK (`MSR_SFMASK = cpu::fmask() = Flags::IF` — verified at the arming site, main.rs:318; the donor's If-only value), so every lock-holding syscall context is uninterruptible; the scheduler runs cli (inherited from the blocked proc; `idle_wait`'s `sti;hlt;cli` holds nothing); the only other sti context is ring 3 (holds no kernel locks). An IRQ-context mcslock acquisition can never meet a holder on this single-CPU cli-disciplined kernel. (The walk remains a latency burden — see §4.)

**C2 — a woken waiter's re-probe driving BOTH cores and consuming the peer's reply: EXONERATED (design + pins).** `drive_stack` iterating both cores is required for progress (ingress dispatches per-4-tuple to the RIGHT socket — a drive ingesting a peer's data is delivery, not theft). The lost-wake corners are covered: every block loop re-probes after its pre-block drive (probe → drive → re-probe → register/arm → block, verified in the tcp/udp/icmp/listen read/write loops), the one-shot waker bridge records into `PENDING_WAKES` and `drive_stack` drains after the STACK guard drops — the exact contract host-pinned by `devip_tests::register_wake_reregister_never_loses_a_wake` (pin (b)) and the Phase-8 wakeup-loss gate history.

**C3 — IRQ-context BusyCell contention: EXONERATED (same invariant as C1).** `NET_WAITERS`/`PENDING_WAKES`/`INGRESS` are taken in IRQ context, but their every holder is syscall-context (cli) — the `.expect(...)` panics are unreachable under the invariant above.

**New evidence N1 — the stale-registration herd (the audit's concrete finding, in the exact CR-07 terrain):** `register_net_waiter` has 10 call sites and **no deregistration counterpart exists anywhere** (grepped; contrast the sem machinery's own `sem_register_waiter`/`sem_deregister_waiter` pairing — CR-03's fix precedent). `NET_WAITERS` therefore accumulates: (a) pids of EXITED procs (benign — pids are monotonic, slots never re-pressed into service, `Moribund ≠ Blocked` in `pipe_wake`), and (b) pids of live procs currently blocked in OTHER subsystems (devpipe/sem/devmnt/cons) — every RX-IRQ/tick wake can SPURIOUSLY kick those. All block loops audited spurious-tolerant (the re-check discipline), so this is not the wedge — but it pollutes the drain-all set and amplifies `pipe_wake`'s per-IRQ work (the wake reaches non-devip contexts; the 512-slot walk scans `pids.contains` per entry) precisely in the launch window where the class fires. It is the one structural divergence from the sem machinery's proven shape in the multi-waiter terrain CR-07 names.

**New evidence N2 — teardown/IRQ/alloc disciplines audited clean at the load-bearing sites:** `exit_tail`'s PML4/kstack free-before-handoff window is cli-closed and TLB-coherent (the code's own comment documents the reasoning; verified against the cli invariant); the RX/TX DMA pages are a closed driver-owned set (`tx_buf_pa`/`rx_buf_pa` + the free stack, V5-validated ids); `FrameStore` is a fixed-slot pool (no IRQ-context allocation); the WR-06 posted-liveness check (17-03) closed the double-retire aliasing hole that WAS open at 16-08.

## 4. The mechanism verdict

**An intermittent, unrecoverable exception-delivery failure (CPU shutdown, triple-fault class) in the burst-launch window — the kernel's highest-churn mixed load: ~16 fork+exec+exit cycles in ~2-3 s (the dc backquote launcher × 8 tcpblast forks) racing the connect-handshake storm (the 8th handshake's completion is the death instant on the wire) under the RX/TX IRQ storm and the 1 ms tick preempt.** No trap dump is possible from this class by definition (the delivery itself fails — the Phase-14 doctrine's "panics lie under corruption" is the governing frame: the corruption is upstream of any print the kernel could make). The specific corrupting writer is not capturable host-side under the KVM ceiling (§2); what the audit excludes is every candidate that the recorded theories named (§3). The surviving mechanism family is transient corruption of exception-delivery state (the Mach-resident IDT/GDT/TSS, a live CR3, or a kstack mapping) — all of which are silent-reset-equivalent — under the launch window's unique interleaving of proc teardown, page recycling, and IRQ delivery.

**The strongest candidate the record can name for the fix (per the plan's own contingency): N1 — the stale-registration herd.** The fix is the sem machinery's own proven shape brought to devip: a `deregister_net_waiter` counterpart called at every block-loop EXIT, making `NET_WAITERS` exactly the set of currently-blocked devip waiters (the drain-all take becomes precise — the plan's "narrowing the drain-all take" example shape verbatim), eliminating the spurious cross-subsystem wake channel and shrinking the IRQ-context wake work in the failing window. It cannot be CLAIMED to cure the corruption class on a logic argument — the plan's own D-03 prohibition — so the fix's evidence is the deterministic pin pair + the stress series, with N restated below.
