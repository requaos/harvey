## Flagged Assumptions (spec-less probe — unresolved edges, do not silently drop)

- **BUILD-05 / boundary** (unresolved): At exactly the boot-budget boundary (60s KVM / 600s TCG) a healthy-but-slow boot fails the assertion; the assumed contract is "rerun with a larger MM_BOOT_BUDGET", documented in the wrapper. One step either side of the budget is not distinguished — the banner grep, not the timer, is the source of truth when the log eventually arrives.
- **BUILD-05 / precision** (unresolved): Serial capture started after `vm start` can lose the early COM1 banner (the kernel prints it within the first milliseconds); the wrapper therefore attaches the socat capture BEFORE starting the VM, and the banner grep tolerates partial-line buffering at capture cutoff. Assumed sufficient; a missed-banner false failure must be triaged manually, not retried silently.
